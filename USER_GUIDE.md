## App Overview

Club Metropolitan is a fitness activities management app that allows you to:
- Browse and search for available activities
- View detailed information about each activity
- Enroll in activities that interest you
- Manage your personal activity schedule
- Handle scheduling conflicts intelligently

## Browsing Activities

### Main Activities Screen

The main screen displays all available activities at the club:

- **Search Bar**: Located at the top of the screen. Use it to filter activities by name, trainer, location, or any other keyword.
- **Activity Cards**: Each card displays basic information about an activity:
  - Activity name
  - Image
  - Time and day
  - Trainer name
  - Location
  - Brief description

Depending on your device, activities will be displayed in either a grid view (for tablets and desktops) or a list view (for mobile phones).

**Tip**: In landscape mode on mobile devices, you'll see more activities at once.

## Viewing Activity Details

Tap on any activity card to open the detailed view:

- **Image**: Full-width image at the top
- **Basic Info**: Name, time, and day
- **Location**: Where the activity will take place
- **Trainer**: Name of the instructor
- **Capacity**: Current enrollment and maximum capacity
- **Description**: Detailed information about the activity
- **Action Button**: At the bottom, allowing you to enroll in the activity

**Tip**: Swipe down to dismiss the detail view, or use the X button in the top-right corner.

## Enrolling in Activities

To enroll in an activity:

1. Open the activity details by tapping on an activity card
2. Review the activity information
3. Tap the "Enroll" button at the bottom of the screen
4. If there are no conflicts, you'll see a confirmation message
5. The activity is now added to your personal schedule

**Note**: The app will automatically check if the activity conflicts with your existing schedule.

## Managing Schedule Conflicts

The app has an intelligent conflict detection system:

- **Conflict Warning**: If you try to enroll in an activity that overlaps with one you're already enrolled in, you'll see a warning message.
- **Options**: You can either:
  - Keep your current enrollment and cancel the new one
  - Change your enrollment by canceling the existing activity and enrolling in the new one

The app displays clear visual indicators:
- Activities you're enrolled in have a green "Enrolled" label
- Activities that conflict with your schedule have an orange "Adjustable" label

## Your Activities

The "My Activities" screen shows all activities you've enrolled in:

- Access it from the main navigation
- View your personal schedule at a glance
- Tap any activity to see details or manage your enrollment
- If you have no activities, you'll see an empty state with an option to explore available activities

To cancel your enrollment in an activity:
1. Open the activity from your activities list
2. Tap the "Cancel Enrollment" button
3. Confirm your choice in the dialog that appears
4. You'll see a confirmation message once the cancellation is complete

## Responsive Design

The app is designed to work seamlessly across different devices:

- **Mobile (Portrait)**: Activities displayed in a single column list
- **Mobile (Landscape)**: Optimized layout with adjusted content positioning
- **Tablet**: Grid view with two or more columns depending on the screen size
- **Desktop**: Multi-column grid with larger cards and more information visible at once

The app automatically adapts to your device's orientation and screen size for the best experience.
